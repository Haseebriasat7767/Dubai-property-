# VÉLORA DUBAI — Brand & Visual Identity Bible

> "Where Architecture Meets Exceptional Living."

VÉLORA DUBAI is a fictional ultra-luxury private development house in Dubai. The brand
communicates architecture, exclusivity, wealth and cinematic restraint — never flash.

---

## 1. Visual Personality

Ultra-premium · Minimal · Architectural · Cinematic · Elegant · Editorial luxury.
A private-development feeling: quiet confidence, deep blacks, warm stone, champagne restraint.

## 2. Color System

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary background | Vélora Noir | `#0B0B0C` | Site background, dark sections |
| Secondary | Deep Noir | `#141416` | Panels, cards |
| Text / light surface | Warm Ivory | `#F4EFE6` | Headlines, light sections |
| Accent (sparingly) | Champagne Gold | `#C6A15B` | Hairlines, CTAs, map markers |
| Supporting | Architectural Gray | `#8A8F98` | Body text on dark, captions |
| Supporting | Bronze | `#6B5B45` | Metal details, secondary accents |

Rule: gold is an accent, never a fill. Maximum ~5% of any composition.

## 3. Typography (recommended for the web build)

- Display: Didone / high-contrast serif — *Cormorant Garamond* (or Didot) for hero titles.
- Text: geometric sans — *Inter* / *Neue Haas Grotesk* with wide tracking for labels.
- Labels are uppercase, letter-spacing ≥ 0.35em.

## 4. Signature Architecture (all VÉLORA properties)

- Stacked floating rectangular volumes, contemporary geometric.
- Warm ivory travertine / limestone cladding, fine horizontal joints.
- Dark bronze architectural metal frames, floor-to-ceiling glass with warm interior glow.
- Deep cantilevered terraces, slim glass balustrades, flat roofs with deep overhangs.
- Long rectangular infinity-edge reflecting pools, mirror-still water.
- Minimal landscaping: rows of tall slender palms, low architectural ground lighting.
- Realistically buildable proportions — never fantasy, never castle.

## 5. Interior Language (Penthouse 01 / Villa / Marine)

Warm ivory travertine floors · dark walnut panelling · honed dark marble · champagne bronze
metal details · sculptural minimal furniture in ivory and charcoal · soft warm indirect
lighting · minimal art · five-star serenity. The same language must appear in every room.

## 6. Cinematic Grade (applies to every image)

Muted sophisticated palette: deep near-black charcoal, warm ivory, champagne gold, soft
architectural gray, subtle bronze. Low saturation, deep shadows, refined contrast,
physically accurate light, realistic glass/water reflections.

## 8. Image Usage Rules (web)

- Heroes: negative space on the left (desktop) or top (mobile) for headline blocks.
- Dark sections sit directly on imagery with a `#0B0B0C` gradient (0 → 70% opacity).
- Section transitions use the `/architecture` and `/backgrounds` stills at low opacity.
- Property cards: 16:9, same photography style as the heroes, title overlay added in the UI only.
